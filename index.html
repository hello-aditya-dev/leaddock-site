You always press send

LeadDock inserts text into the composer. It never auto-sends, never bulk-messages, and never scrapes message history. Your account stays yours.

  1. Install

    Load the extension in Chrome from your download — two minutes, no account needed.

  2. Open WhatsApp Web

    The LeadDock panel appears beside your active conversation automatically.

  3. Capture context

    Statuses, tags, notes and follow-ups attach to the contact as you chat.

  4. Follow up & close

    The Today dashboard tells you who's waiting. Insert a saved reply and send it yourself.

Your data stays local. On purpose.

No backend. No cloud sync. No telemetry. CRM records live in your browser's local storage — export CSV or JSON backups whenever you like.

What LeadDock reads

Only the current chat header (to identify the contact) and the composer element (to insert text).

What it stores

Contact details, notes, tags, statuses and replies — in chrome.storage.local, on your machine.

What it transmits

Nothing. There is no server to transmit to. See our full privacy policy.
